# Model robustness comparison

## Robustness definition

Robustness is normalized on a 0–100 scale.

- For `jaccard` and `iou_buffer`, robustness is `100 * score`.
  A value of 100 means identical to the baseline.
- For utility ratio metrics, robustness is `100 * min(ratio, 1 / ratio)`.
  This treats increases and decreases from the baseline symmetrically.
- `mean_robustness_pct` summarizes the average robustness.
- `min_robustness_pct` captures the worst observed robustness.
- `max_drop_pct` is `100 - min_robustness_pct`.
- `slope_pct_per_level` estimates whether robustness decreases as the noise level increases.
